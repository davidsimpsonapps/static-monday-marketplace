# App review checklist

The app review checklist contains all the app submission requirements for the marketplace. It can help you prepare for the app review process and save time in the long run.

We highly recommend reviewing this checklist long before submitting your app to our team. Otherwise, you may need to make significant changes during the review process, face delays, or be rejected outright.

# Categories

The checklist is comprised of seven different categories that we will use to evaluate your app. You can find the relevant requirements for each category on the following pages:

* [app listing page](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/app-listing-page)
* [documentation and support ](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/documentation-and-support)
* [legal](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/legal)
* [partnership](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/partnership)
* [privacy and security](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/privacy-and-security)
* [product](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/product)
* [UI/UX](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/uiux)

# Process

1. Complete the checklist by ensuring your app meets the requirements for all seven categories.
2. [Submit your app](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/submit-your-app#submit-your-app-for-review) to the marketplace.
3. Your app will undergo the [app review process](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/submit-your-app#app-review-process). Our team will determine whether or not your app incorporates all the required elements. You will work collaboratively with them on a shared board to address any issues in your submission.
4. Once our team approves your app, we will list it in the marketplace!
