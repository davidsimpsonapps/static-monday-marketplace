# Best practices

Tips to make customers trust your integration app.

As you're developing your integration, do the following.

# Host your apps on trusted infrastructure

The infrastructure you choose to host your app on can have a big impact on if customers adopt your app, especially upmarket customers.

If you're an independent developer, we recommend using monday code hosting. This will give your app the same level of security as the core monday plaform, with little work from you.

If you're an ISV or product developer, you should host your integration on either monday code or servers that are covered by your app's terms.

You can also deploy your app to the [same data region as your customers.](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/multi-region-feature)

# Prepare for the marketplace review early

If your app will be distributed in the monday app marketplace, prepare your app's marketplace materials early. Don't wait until the last minute – preparing your materials in parallel to development will get your app to market as fast as possible.

* Create your app's listing graphics, video, and description
* Ensure your app contains at least 1 app feature
* Review our [security requirements](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/privacy-and-security), such as protecting against injection attacks and maintaining the relevant SSL certificates
* Fill out the optional [Security & Compliance questionaire](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/security-and-compliance)
