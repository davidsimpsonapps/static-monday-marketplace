# Legal

Learn about the legal requirements for listing an app in the marketplace

To receive approval for the app marketplace, your app must meet the following legal requirements:

* [ ] Create a unique app name up to 30 characters long. **Important:** monday.com legal cannot endorse the usage of monday or monday.com in the app name.
* [ ] Provide a full contact name.
* [ ] List your company/entity name.
* [ ] Share a link to your *Terms of Service*. This will appear on the app card in the marketplace, allowing customers to easily access it. Your agreement must clearly state whether you plan on contacting users in the future.
* [ ] Make your privacy policy publicly available and provide a link. You must list the privacy policy under the same entity name you provided.
