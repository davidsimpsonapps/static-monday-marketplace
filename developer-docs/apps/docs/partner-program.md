# Partner Program

The monday app marketplace Partner Program helps support and empower our app developers on their journey toward success. Through this initiative, we strive to provide a collaborative community that nurtures innovation, amplifies exposure, and drives mutual growth.

With a range of benefits and resources tailored to their unique needs, our Partner Program is the key to unlocking endless possibilities. This guide will walk you through the program requirements, benefits, and how you can become a partner. Let's get started!

# Requirements

The Marketplace Partner Program is divided into four tiers: *Platinum*, *Gold*, *Silver*, and *Bronze*. In addition to the requirements all apps must fulfill to be listed in the marketplace (see more [here](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/app-review-checklist)), each tier has additional requirements that your app must meet to participate in the program.

Below, you can find a full breakdown of the requirements according to tier.

| Requirements                                                                                                | Bronze     | Silver     | Gold       | Platinum   |
| :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------- | :--------- | :--------- | :--------- |
| [Apps rating](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/marketplace-app-ratings-and-reviews)                   | -          | 4+         | 4+         | 4.5+       |
| [Monetized by monday](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/monetization)                                  | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        |
| Customer Service Level Agreement (SLA)                                                                      | < 48 hours | < 48 hours | < 24 hours | < 24 hours |
| [Security Questionnaire](https://forms.monday.com/forms/1e126005a08c78a19ceac5b810fae8b3?r=use1)            | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        |
| Minimum marketplace annual recurring revenue (ARR) from monday.com native monetization                      | $5K        | $75K       | $300K      | $1M        |
| Sign [listing agreement](https://monday.com/l/marketplace-developers/monday-com-marketplace-listing-terms/) | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        |
| Complete the [app review process](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/submit-your-app)                   | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        | Yes        |

*\*Quarterly performance evaluations will be conducted to ensure that all requirements are met.*
