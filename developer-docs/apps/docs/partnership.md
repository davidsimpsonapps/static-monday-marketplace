# Partnership

Learn about the partnership requirements for listing an app in the marketplace

To receive approval for the app marketplace, your app must meet the following partnership requirements:

* [ ] Embed a button on your website to install your app. You can explore ways to share your app by navigating to **Developers** > **Your app** > **Share**.
* [ ] Once your app has been approved, share a post on LinkedIn using our Figma designs and your app installation link. While not required, this post can help your app get more traffic!
* [ ] Optionally, provide feedback about your app-building experience with us through this [form](https://forms.monday.com/forms/4f494ce7fdc96f58b05cf46848fdfdec?r=use1)!
