# Reviews

Learn how to view and manage your app’s ratings, reviews, and customer feedback trends

The *Reviews* tab in the [Developer Center](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/the-developer-center) displays your app's [ratings and reviews](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/marketplace-app-ratings-and-reviews). Here, you can view your app's average rating, explore individual reviews, and analyze user feedback trends.

Marketplace ratings and reviews are key indicators of your app's quality, reliability, and user satisfaction. Analyzing this data helps you:

* Identify areas for improvement
* Build trust and credibility
* Better understand your customers
* Prioritize fixes or new features based on feedback

# Export the Data

You can export data from most widgets on the *Reviews* page as a CSV file for further analysis or sharing.

To export:

1. Navigate to the *Reviews* tab.
2. Click the **three dots (…)** in the top-right corner of the relevant widget.
3. Select **Download CSV**.
4. Open the CSV file from your local device.
