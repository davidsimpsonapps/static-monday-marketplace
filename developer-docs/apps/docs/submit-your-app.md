# Submit your app to the marketplace

The [marketplace](https://monday.com/marketplace) is a one-stop shop for third-party apps built on top of monday to bridge critical gaps and optimize workflows. Any app developer can build and list an app in the marketplace to reach hundreds of thousands of monday.com customers.

All apps must go through a four-phase process before receiving marketplace approval. This guide outlines the requirements for each phase to ensure a smooth and efficient experience.

# Phase 1: Prepare for submission

In the first phase, you must ensure that your app meets all marketplace requirements. If any unmet requirements are identified, they will be flagged during the review process.

<Callout icon="🚧" theme="warn">
  New apps built primarily using no-code platforms or AI-generated “vibe code” are not eligible for marketplace approval.
</Callout>

1. Complete the [app listing guidelines](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/app-listing-guidelines) and [app review checklist](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/app-review-checklist).
2. Review the following policies and terms to better understand your requirements and expectations:
   1. [monday.com Marketplace Listing Terms](https://monday.com/l/legal/monday-com-marketplace-listing-terms/)
   2. [monday.com Developer Terms](https://monday.com/l/legal/developer-terms/)
   3. [monday.com Privacy Policy](https://monday.com/terms/privacy)
   4. [monday.com Terms of Service](https://monday.com/terms/tos)
3. Consider the following questions:
   1. Is your app a valid monday app?
   2. Are you ready to support your future users in line with the monday.com marketplace SLA?
   3. Will you continuously ensure that your app meets monday standards while listed in the marketplace?

# Phase 2: Submit your app

After completing phase one, you can submit your app by following these steps:

1. Open the [Developer Center](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/the-developer-center#access-the-developer-center).
2. Click the **Share** tab and publish your app. This creates a shareable link that starts with *auth.monday.com*, which is required to complete the submission form. If you've already published your app, skip to the next step. Once you have the link, open the **Submit app** tab.
3. Click **Submission form** and complete the required sections. You can also access the form directly [here](https://forms.monday.com/forms/96abe3d41fa02cf8b4c8128f28432869?r=use1).

# Phase 3: Review process

After submission, your app moves to the review process. Our dedicated app review team will evaluate your app against the criteria outlined in phase one, assessing the product, engineering, security, privacy, content, assets, support, documentation, and legal aspects.

If any requirements are unmet, our team will communicate them through a monday.com board. You will receive an invitation to this board via email so you can easily collaborate with the review team. Once you've addressed all of the flagged issues, the team will reevaluate your app and grant your marketplace approval.

You can expect an initial response within 72 business hours of submission. After that, the duration of this phase depends on the app type, size, features, and how quickly you make progress on the review board items.

Please note that submissions that do not demonstrate clear, unique value to monday.com users or significantly replicate the functionality of existing marketplace apps may be declined. If your app is not approved, we encourage you to explore fresh, innovative ideas that deliver meaningful value and enhanced capabilities to monday.com users

# Phase 4: After approval

Upon approval, your app enters and remains in the marketplace lifecycle. You can leverage our native tools to:

* Monitor your app's [analytics](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/analytics)
* Manage user [reviews](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/marketplace-app-ratings-and-reviews)
* [Update your app](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/update-your-app)
* Create new [app versions](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/app-versioning)

Ongoing maintenance is essential to ensure marketplace compliance and provide a good user experience!
