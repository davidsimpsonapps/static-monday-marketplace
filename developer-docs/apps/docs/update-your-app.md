# Update your app

As your app evolves over time, you may need to make changes to its functionality, content, marketplace listing, pricing, or app features. Each part of your app follows a slightly different process to make relevant updates and changes.

Check out each of the following documents to learn how to update the respective parts:

* [App listing page](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/update-your-app-listing-page)
* [Marketplace pricing](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/plans-and-pricing#pricing-versioning)
* [Permissions and scopes](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/app-versioning#update-permissions-or-scopes)
* [Security and compliance](https://developer.monday.com/apps/docs/update-your-app-security-and-compliance)
