---
title: "monday.com postpones the October 1 deadline for the new OAuth migration"
date: 2026-09-10
topic: community
lede: "monday.com has postponed the October 1 deadline for migrating apps to its new OAuth flow, citing the number of issues and questions raised by developers. A new deadline will follow once the problems are resolved."
---

The new deadline will be set once the new OAuth version is considered stable and becomes the default, following the same lifecycle as monday.com's platform API versions. monday.com said that it keeps working on the new OAuth flow, and that it would share an estimated timeline for the fixes developers had raised the following week.

*Source: monday.com in the #oauth-2-1 channel of the monday developers community Slack*
