---
title: "Storydoc app creates personalized decks from monday workflows"
date: 2026-09-11
topic: new-apps
lede: "Storydoc Technologies has released Storydoc for monday.com, which creates personalized Storydoc presentations from board data when a workflow triggers."
---

[Storydoc](/apps/10001274/) maps monday columns such as name, company or deal size to variables in a Storydoc template, and creates a deck when an item is created, a deal reaches a stage or another workflow condition is met. It is aimed at sales, marketing, customer success and partnership teams. The app is free to install but requires a Storydoc account, which is paid after a 14-day trial.
