[Zoom Team Chat for monday](/apps/10001269/) posts a card with a link back to the item when items are created, changed, moved, updated or deleted, filtered by group, column and value. It also sends daily or weekly board digests and reminders for due, overdue and stalled items, and can route each rule to a different channel. It uses Zoom's Incoming Webhook, so no Zoom login is required.
