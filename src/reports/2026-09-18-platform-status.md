### Search

The [UI functionality incident](https://stspg.io/cmtlwrc36jrn) affected some UI features, including Search, which was marked as a partial outage in the US, EU, AUS and IL regions. A fix was deployed at 10:43 UTC and the incident was resolved at 11:05 UTC; monday.com advised refreshing the browser.

### Automations and integrations

In the EU region, [automations, workflows and integrations](https://stspg.io/xxv13qy962yr) failed or would not load, marked as a major outage. A fix was deployed at 10:25 UTC and the incident was resolved at 10:39 UTC.
