title: "Item to Form and WorkCurrent AI for monday join the marketplace"
date: 2026-09-23
topic: new-apps
lede: "Two new apps: Item to Form from Oran Sherf creates public forms that write responses back to items, and WorkCurrent AI for monday adds a sales workspace and AI assistant to monday CRM."
---

[Item to Form](/apps/10001250/) generates a shareable form from an item, so people without a monday.com account, such as vendors, clients or applicants, can fill in fields that are written back to the item. Forms support validation, read-only fields, passwords and expiration dates, and can be created in batches or from automations. The vendor states that the app is hosted entirely on monday.com.

[WorkCurrent AI for monday](/apps/10001275/) adds stages, required fields and key questions to existing monday CRM deal boards. Its AI assistant reviews calls, notes, messages and emails from connected tools and can update deal fields, stages and notes; the app also adds forecast dashboards, pre-call briefs and drafted follow-ups. It is aimed at mid-sized B2B sales teams.
