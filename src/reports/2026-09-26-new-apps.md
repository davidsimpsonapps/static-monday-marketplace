date: 2026-09-26

lede: "Boost Plugins has released QuickBooks Desktop Integration, which embeds an Accounting Relay dashboard in monday.com for copying QuickBooks Desktop records to boards."
---

[QuickBooks Desktop Integration](/apps/10001278/) lets teams browse bills, invoices and other accounting documents from QuickBooks Desktop and export selected records into a board, with configurable field-to-column mapping. The connection to QuickBooks Desktop runs through Accounting Relay, a separate third-party service that the app does not set up. A subscription is required to set up the connector.
