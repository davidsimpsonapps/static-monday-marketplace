date: 2026-09-30
topic: new-apps
lede: "OOMOL has released an app that makes monday.com actions available to agents and workflows on its platform."
---

With [OOMOL](/apps/10001279/), agents can read and update boards and items, summarize item updates, post follow-ups, create docs, work with files and resolve users and teams, within the connected account's permissions and OAuth scopes. Team administrators can limit a connection to selected members and to selected actions, for example read-only access for reporting.
