---

[Factura ARCA e-Invoicing](/apps/10001256/) from The Automation Partner connects boards to ARCA, Argentina's tax authority (formerly AFIP), and issues A, B and C invoices and credit notes when a status column changes. It maps clients, CUITs and line items, including subitems, writes the CAE back to the board, attaches a PDF with QR code and checks a client's tax status before issuing.

[Sales Radar AI](/apps/10001281/) from appserve.ai, a division of Empyra, uses OpenAI and Perplexity AI to suggest leads that match an ideal customer profile, recommend next steps and track competitor activity such as website changes, product launches and campaigns. It is aimed at sales and marketing teams using monday CRM.
