title: "monday.com raises the bar for new marketplace apps and outlines its review criteria"
date: 2026-10-06
topic: community
lede: "monday.com is raising the bar for the apps it publishes and promotes in the marketplace, and has described what it looks for when reviewing new submissions."
---

Going forward, monday.com is primarily looking for apps that meet one or more of these criteria: they fill a meaningful gap in what monday.com covers today, deliver clear value through a strong use case and a tangible benefit for end users, or add AI capabilities that go beyond existing native functionality. Apps that build AI experiences consuming monday.com AI credits are preferred.

Differentiation also counts: monday.com wants to avoid adding apps that mainly replicate functionality already in monday.com, or already well covered by existing marketplace apps. It plans to share a list of the key product gaps and customer needs it currently sees, and pointed developers building AI apps to its update on [consuming AI models through monday](https://developer.monday.com/api-reference/changelog/consume-ai-models-through-monday) (API version 2026-10).
