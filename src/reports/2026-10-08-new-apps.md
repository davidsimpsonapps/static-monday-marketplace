lede: "[Formula Column Automation](/apps/10001284/) from Satisfaction Drivers is a new marketplace app. It writes the results of formula columns into editable columns, so they can be used as emails, phone numbers, URLs or coordinates."
---

Users pick a source formula column, a target column and an output format, such as text, email, phone, URL, coordinates or numbers. Optional formatting rules cover text case, number padding and regional phone formats. The app syncs the board once, then updates the target column when the underlying data changes.
