date: 2026-10-09

lede: "StatusTrail reports time in status and cycle time, including for subitems. Heymarket connects monday.com boards to the Heymarket business texting platform."
---

[StatusTrail](/apps/10001276/), from Lucas REYGROBELLET, is a board view that shows how long items and subitems spend in each status, with cycle time and an optional business-hours mode. It can export the data to CSV and shows the transition history behind each duration. Its only permission is `boards:read`.

[Heymarket](/apps/10001287/), from Heymarket, syncs board items to Heymarket as contacts so teams can send SMS and MMS texts. Messages are posted back to the item as updates. It requires an active Heymarket account, which has a 14-day free trial and then a paid plan.
