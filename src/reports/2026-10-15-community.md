---
title: "monday API client 14.2.0 adds idempotency key support to SeamlessApiClient"
date: 2026-10-15
topic: community
lede: "monday.com released `@mondaydotcomorg/api@14.2.0`, which extends idempotency key support to `SeamlessApiClient`. Version 14.1.0 had added it to `ApiClient` earlier."
---

In a thread in the developers-hub channel, monday.com said on October 6 that `@mondaydotcomorg/api@14.1.0` added support for idempotency keys in `ApiClient`.

In a later reply, monday.com said that as of `@mondaydotcomorg/api@14.2.0`, `SeamlessApiClient` also supports idempotency keys. The posts give no further details on usage.

*Source: monday.com in the monday developers community Slack*
