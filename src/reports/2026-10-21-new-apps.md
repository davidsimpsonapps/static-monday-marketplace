title: "Three new apps: Salesmate, Folk and ZeroBounce"
date: 2026-10-21
topic: new-apps
lede: "Two CRM integrations from Codex Solutions International and an email validation app from ZeroBounce have been added to the marketplace."
---

[Salesmate](/apps/10001280/) connects monday.com boards to Salesmate CRM. Automations can create, update and delete contacts, deals and activities in Salesmate and save returned IDs back to board items.

[Folk](/apps/10001289/) does the same for Folk CRM, covering People and Companies. It also offers triggers that run monday.com automations when a new Person or Company is created in Folk.

[ZeroBounce](/apps/10001294/) validates and scores email addresses for monday.com CRM and monday Campaigns lists. It can verify new contacts as they are added and run scheduled cleaning.
