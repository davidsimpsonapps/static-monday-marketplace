title: "Related Items matches board items by shared key without connect limits"
date: 2026-10-26
topic: new-apps
lede: "[Related Items](/apps/10001293/) by AllDayIT Ltd. is a new marketplace app that relates high-volume data to items through a shared key, such as an account number or SKU, instead of connect or mirror columns."
---

The app calculates totals, counts and dates into ordinary Numbers and Date columns, and offers an item view, extra board views, exports and AI-written item briefings. It is aimed at sales, finance, operations and HR teams.
