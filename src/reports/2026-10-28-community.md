title: "Non-monday-code apps using Node.js must patch within 48 hours, monday.com says"
date: 2026-10-28
topic: community
lede: "monday.com has told developers of apps not hosted on monday code that they will get an email about a Node.js issue. Apps that use Node.js must be patched within 48 hours, and replies go through a form, not by email."
---

The announcement in the #announcements channel is addressed to apps that do not run on monday code. It says these developers will also receive an email about the matter soon. The post does not describe what the patch is for.

Developers who don't use Node.js can reply that the notice is not relevant to them. Those who do use it are required to patch their app within 48 hours.

monday.com asks developers to respond using [this form](https://wkf.ms/4il0s9q) and not to reply to the email. The editors' note says the announcement applies from Monday, October 26.

*Source: monday.com in the monday developers community Slack*
