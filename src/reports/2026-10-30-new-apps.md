date: 2026-10-30
topic: new-apps
lede: "Auto Assign by Mikada and Onro by MB Onro Technologies appeared in the monday.com marketplace."
---

[Auto Assign](/apps/10000242/) provides assignee automations for boards. They assign or add the person who changes a status, or remove that person from the people column.

[Onro](/apps/10001297/) syncs customer data from the Onro courier and delivery software into monday CRM. It writes to existing Contacts and Accounts boards, or creates its own Onro Contacts and Onro Accounts boards if they don't exist. It is aimed at courier, logistics and last-mile delivery companies.
