date: 2026-10-31
topic: new-apps
lede: "Achievement Hub turns board activity into XP and leaderboards, and Item to Form generates public forms from monday items. Both are new in the marketplace."
---

[Achievement Hub](/apps/10001237/), from Alicorn Hub, is a board view that reads activity such as completed items, status changes, comments and likes, and converts it into XP, levels, achievements and leaderboards. It includes five workflow templates, and admins can adjust XP weights and achievement settings. It says it runs inside monday.com using monday-managed storage.

[Item to Form](/apps/10001250/), from Prodactive Software, lets teams generate a public form from a board item and send the link to people outside the organization. Respondents need no monday.com account, and their answers are written back to the item. It supports field validation, password protection, expiration dates and batch form creation.
