date: 2026-11-07

lede: "[Community Cookbook](/apps/10001128/), from SLG Limited, is a new marketplace app that extends monday.com automations with extra triggers and actions and a JavaScript code editor."
---

The app's triggers cover cases such as mirror and formula column changes, subitem status thresholds and approval decisions. Its actions work on columns, subitems, boards and dates, for example copying values between columns, bulk-changing subitem statuses and exporting a board to Excel. It is aimed at users who need automation logic beyond monday.com's built-in options.
