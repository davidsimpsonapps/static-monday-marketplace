lede: "[Bulk Edit + Undo](/apps/10001290/), from RVDH Interim Management B.V., is a new marketplace app for changing one column value across many items at once and reverting the change in one click."
---

The app is aimed at teams with large boards, such as project trackers, sales pipelines and support queues. Users select items, apply a value to a column, and can restore all changed items to their previous values. Frequently used column and value combinations can be saved as reusable rules.
