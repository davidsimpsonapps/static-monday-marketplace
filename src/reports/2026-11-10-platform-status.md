### Service disruption on US server

The [major incident](https://stspg.io/k2w49kglyrff) affected the US Automations, Dashboards and Platform components. monday.com first reported platform loading and connectivity problems, with some services possibly unavailable in other locations too. A fix was implemented at 16:03 UTC, and slowness with dashboards, automations and the API was expected during recovery. The platform and automations were restored at 16:42 UTC, and the incident was marked resolved at 17:30 UTC. Users were asked to refresh their browser.

### My Work and Dashboards not loading

The [minor incident](https://stspg.io/7c8c2sqvlv9j) affected the US Dashboards component. monday.com said some US users had trouble loading My Work and Dashboards due to maintenance. A fix for dashboards was implemented at 20:23 UTC, and the incident was resolved at 21:04 UTC.

Current and past status data is on the [service status page](/status/).
