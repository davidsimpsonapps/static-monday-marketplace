title: "Ten new apps appear in the marketplace, including three-app Satisfaction Drivers embed set"
date: 2026-11-12
topic: new-apps
lede: "Ten apps were added to the marketplace, covering subitem editing, embeds for Loom, Trello, Google Forms, Canva and Google Calendar, column automations, ad metrics sync and email approvals."
---

[Better Subitems](/apps/10000687/) by Sharply lets users view, add, edit and remove subitems from within item views. It supports Text, Status, People and Item Name columns.

Satisfaction Drivers published five apps: [Loom Embed for monday](/apps/10000753/) embeds Loom videos in boards, items, docs and dashboards; [Trello Embed for monday.com](/apps/10000760/) embeds Trello and syncs cards; [Google Forms Integration for monday.com](/apps/10000786/) embeds Google Forms; [Canva Embedder for monday.com](/apps/10000795/) embeds Canva designs; and [Google Calendar for monday.com](/apps/10000808/) syncs Google Calendar with boards and embeds it in views.

[GenieTools](/apps/10000996/) by Holistic Data Solutions adds automations beyond native limits, such as copying column values, assigning people from a master board, finding duplicate items, removing "(copy)" suffixes and counting files.

[GetSet Sync](/apps/10001202/) by Bulltehead lifestyle syncs Google Ads and Meta Ads metrics into board columns on a schedule, aimed at marketing teams and agencies.

Astro Digital published two apps. [SimplyApprove](/apps/10001235/) adds an automation action that emails an approver Approve and Decline links, so approvers need no monday.com account. [SimplyTrigger](/apps/10001252/) adds an automation recipe that changes a status column when a numeric formula column crosses a threshold.
