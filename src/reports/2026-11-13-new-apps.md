### Automations and data entry

[Update to Columns](/apps/10000383/) by Unitask Ltd extracts data from item updates and writes it into columns. [Required Columns](/apps/10000396/) by Reqfields marks columns as required and uses automations to set a status, move items between groups or revert them until those columns are filled; an item view shows which ones are missing. [Repeating Tasks for Monday.com](/apps/10000415/) by 123X creates and schedules recurring tasks. [Round Robin Pro](/apps/10000488/) by Kolaai assigns items to users or teams by round robin or weighted distribution, taking working hours, time zones and absences into account. [Snooze item](/apps/10000513/) by GIKI Technologies hides items until a set time, when they reappear on the board.

[Quick Copy & Move Files](/apps/10000825/) by Inception Software LLP copies, moves, syncs and renames files between file columns, items and boards. [Bulk Update Pro](/apps/10001068/) by Leon Gael LLC edits many items at once, with a preview before changes are applied. [Workflow Hub](/apps/10000843/) by Capable defines allowed transitions for a status column in a visual editor, so users only see valid next steps.

### Documents, embeds and integrations

ShortDay published two apps: [ShortURL](/apps/10000435/) creates short, memorable links to boards, and [Doc2Board for monday](/apps/10000506/) uses OCR and AI to turn PDF, DOC and TXT files into items. MarketForge published three: [BoardCast](/apps/10000774/) sends WhatsApp and Telegram messages to contacts on a board, [SheetEmbed Google Sheets Embed](/apps/10000811/) embeds editable Google Sheets, and [DocEmbed - Google Docs Embed](/apps/10000877/) embeds Google Docs in items, boards and dashboards.

[Woocommerce By Luxie Tech](/apps/10000533/) by Luxie Tech syncs WooCommerce orders and customers into monday.com. [Shared Mailer](/apps/10000978/) by Osnetic Corp sends HTML emails from boards, including from passwordless shared mailboxes, with column values as placeholders.

### Views and reporting

[More Charts](/apps/10000640/) by Grey Bottle adds over 20 chart types with appearance settings. [MyWeek](/apps/10000870/) by Virtual Vines is a calendar that combines items from several boards to plan a team's workload. [Column Groups](/apps/10000997/) by Asplund Software AB adds a board view that sorts columns into named, collapsible groups without changing the board. Leon Gael LLC also published [Smart Row Highlighter](/apps/10001043/), which colors rows based on rules about column values, and [Board Analytics](/apps/10001075/), which shows completion rates, status and group distribution and overdue items for a board.
