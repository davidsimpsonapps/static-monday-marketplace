---
title: "Microsoft outage disrupts monday.com Outlook automations and Notetaker"
date: 2026-11-13
topic: platform-status
lede: "monday.com posted a minor incident affecting automations that involve Outlook and its Notetaker, caused by a Microsoft outage. It started on 12 November at 21:05 UTC and was not yet resolved."
---

According to the [incident](https://stspg.io/l05sfwvbnq25), new or rescheduled Outlook meetings may not get the Notetaker bot, and automations involving Outlook are affected. The degraded components were Automations in the AUS and US regions.

At 21:13 UTC monday.com said it had identified the root cause and was waiting for Microsoft to resolve the issue on its side.

Current service health is on the [service status page](/status/).
