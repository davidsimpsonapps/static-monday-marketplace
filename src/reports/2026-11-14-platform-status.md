---
title: "Microsoft outage disrupted monday automations and Notetaker for Outlook, now resolved"
date: 2026-11-14
topic: platform-status
lede: "monday.com resolved an incident at 09:47 UTC on 13 November that affected Outlook-related automations and Notetaker, caused by a Microsoft outage. It began at 21:05 UTC on 12 November."
---

The [status page incident](https://stspg.io/l05sfwvbnq25) was rated minor. Affected components were Automations and Integrations in the AUS, EU and US regions. New or rescheduled Outlook meetings might not get the Notetaker bot, and automations involving Outlook were affected.

monday.com identified the root cause at 21:13 UTC as the Microsoft issue and waited for Microsoft to fix it. At 09:47 UTC it reported the Microsoft 365 issue resolved. Some delays in Microsoft-related automations remained on the AUS server while its queue caught up.

Current state is on the [service status page](/status/).
