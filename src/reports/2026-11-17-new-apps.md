lede: "ProofNow, Ripple Merge and MasterForms AI: Advanced Forms have appeared in the marketplace. They cover creative proofing, email mail merge and public forms."
---

[ProofNow](/apps/10001299/), from Workflow Boost, turns monday items into proofing and approval hubs for marketing, creative and agency teams. Internal and external reviewers can annotate designs, videos and documents, and approvals run in staged workflows.

[Ripple Merge](/apps/10001300/), from CODERIPPLE TECH LTD, adds mail merge to monday emails, including subitem data that built-in email automations cannot use. It offers reusable templates with loops, filters and totals, plus optional AI writing and translation.

[MasterForms AI: Advanced Forms](/apps/10001303/), from Fortimus, builds public forms that create board items or update existing items and subitems. It supports conditional logic, PDF output with e-signatures and multi-step approvals.
