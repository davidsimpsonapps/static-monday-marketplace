The [incident](https://stspg.io/sylthlf9pyz7) affected the Emails & Activities component in the AUS, EU, IL and US regions. Some users had problems with the Reply All button and recipients. The issue was identified at 16:43 UTC.

At 17:24 UTC monday.com said the feature was working normally again. See the [service status page](/status/) for more.
