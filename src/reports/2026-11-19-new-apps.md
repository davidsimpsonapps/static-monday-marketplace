[EasySend for monday.com](/apps/10000930/) is an automation and workflow platform for collecting data, approvals and eSignatures from customers. It supports multi-party journeys such as onboarding, claims and agreements, and is aimed at teams that want to run these processes and keep the results in monday.com.
