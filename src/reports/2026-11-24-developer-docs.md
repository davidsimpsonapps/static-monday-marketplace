The updated section lists `Meeting` fields including `title`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `recording_duration`, `summary`, `access_type`, `meeting_link` and `series_id`. `series_id` is described as the ID of the recurring calendar series a meeting belongs to, and is `null` for one-off meetings.

See the [Notetaker reference](https://developer.monday.com/api-reference/reference/notetaker) for the current field list.
