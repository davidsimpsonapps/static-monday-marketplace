---

[Meeting Agenda](/apps/10001193/) is a board view. Users pick items, order them, set durations, a title and a start time. Agendas can hold up to 100 items and can be copied into an email or document, or exported as a PDF. A full-screen Present Mode shows one item at a time, with notes and the time it should end. Present Mode requires the Pro plan.
