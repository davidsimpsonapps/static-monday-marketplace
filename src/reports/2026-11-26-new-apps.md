---

[Get Status Changer](/apps/10000745/) records who last changed a Status column and assigns that user to a People column through an automation.

[Timeline manipulator](/apps/10000748/) updates timeline columns when a status changes, a person is assigned or an item is created. It sets the timeline to today's date or moves it forward by a set number of days.

[ExtraflowDoc Quotes & eSign](/apps/10001285/) lets sales teams create quotes, proposals, invoices and other documents from CRM deal data, and collect e-signatures on them.
