date: 2026-11-27

lede: "[Copy Files Pro](/apps/10000764/) by CloudVandana is a new app for copying, syncing, moving and renaming files between items and boards."
---

It automates file transfers that would otherwise be done by downloading and re-uploading, including copying files from item updates to the Files column. It works through automation recipes and monday.com triggers, and is aimed at project, operations, marketing and sales teams.
